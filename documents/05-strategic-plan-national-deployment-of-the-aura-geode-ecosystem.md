## Strategic Plan: National Deployment of the Aura Geode Ecosystem

## Executive Summary

This document presents a comprehensive strategic plan for the national deployment of 70,000 'Made in Australia' Aura Geode Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy (HBOT) units, supported by a $2.5 billion budget. The mission is to establish the Aura Geode ecosystem as the foundation of a national, community-owned preventative health and digital twin infrastructure, thereby democratising access to advanced longevity technology for all Australians. This initiative is architected to capitalise on a unique confluence of societal needs and technological capabilities, positioning Australia as a global leader in preventative healthcare and sovereign medical manufacturing.

The strategy is built upon four integrated pillars: a novel dual manufacturing model designed for rapid market saturation and strategic repurposing, a cooperative community-owned business architecture, a Decentralised Autonomous Organisation (DAO) powered financial engine, and a clinically-driven phased national rollout. The manufacturing framework combines the economies of scale of centralised, highly automated industrial facilities for core component production with the agility and community integration of a network of decentralised regional workshops for final assembly, customisation, and servicing. This approach ensures cost-efficiency while fostering local skills and economic resilience.

The $2.5 billion budget is strategically allocated across key operational areas, including Centralised Manufacturing Capital Expenditure (CAPEX), Decentralised Workshop Seeding, Regulatory and Clinical Trial execution, Cooperative Loan Subsidies, and ongoing Research and Development (R&D). The plan outlines a clear path to achieving the target unit cost of AUD $35,000, an important factor for ensuring widespread accessibility.

The primary Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) for success are the deployment of 70,000 units within a five-year timeframe, the achievement of the target unit cost, successful Therapeutic Goods Administration (TGA) and Human Research Ethics Committee (HREC) approvals, and the establishment of a financially self-sustaining national network of member-owned cooperatives.

The important path for this project is clear and immediate. The core recommendation is to prioritise a front-loaded investment into a comprehensive Design for Manufacturing and Assembly (DFMA) process to validate the unit cost model and produce a manufacturing-ready prototype. Concurrently, the initiation of the Queensland-based clinical trial is paramount, as it serves as the linchpin for unlocking both regulatory approval and market access, establishing the clinical credibility required for national adoption. This strategic plan provides the definitive roadmap for transforming a visionary concept into a tangible, high-impact national asset.

## Section I: The Strategic Imperative and National Opportunity

The Aura Geode initiative is architected as a direct response to a convergence of powerful macro-trends that create a unique and compelling market opportunity. The project is not merely an incremental innovation but a fundamental reimagining of healthcare delivery, ownership, and finance, strategically positioned to address pressing national challenges while leveraging emergent technological paradigms.

## 1.1 A Confluence of Macro-Trends

The viability and timeliness of this national deployment are underpinned by three powerful and simultaneous shifts in the socio-economic and technological landscape.

## The Demographic Imperative

Australia, in line with other developed nations, is experiencing a significant demographic shift towards an ageing population. This creates an unprecedented and rapidly expanding market for preventative health and longevity solutions. The Aura Geode project directly serves this national imperative by strategically shifting the healthcare focus from the reactive treatment of sickness to the proactive cultivation of wellness and an extended healthspan. By making advanced preventative therapies accessible, the ecosystem provides a scalable infrastructure to manage the long-term health of the population, potentially reducing the future burden on the public healthcare system.

## Erosion of Trust in Centralised Systems

A well-documented erosion of public trust in governments, financial institutions, and traditional healthcare systems creates a significant opening for decentralised, transparent, and community-owned alternatives. The cooperative business model, owned and governed by its members rather than by external shareholders, offers a compelling governance structure that aligns directly with this societal shift. Combined with the transparent and auditable nature of the GAJRA Earth DAO, this framework is presented as a more resilient, member-aligned, and trustworthy system for managing an important community health asset. It represents a "third way" between the extractive tendencies of pure capitalism and the potential inefficiencies of state-run programs, empowering communities through self-help and equitable ownership. It rejects the infinite growth model by focusing on saturating a community need and then pivoting the established infrastructure to the next challenge, embodying a truly regenerative and adaptive economic cycle. 1

## The Technological Singularity

The entire Aura Geode ecosystem would have been technically infeasible a decade ago. Its architecture is made possible only through the recent and simultaneous maturation of several distinct technologies. The confluence of advanced AI for processing vast streams of biometric data, blockchain for providing trustless governance and financial infrastructure, and advanced manufacturing for producing complex hardware at scale forms the project's technological bedrock. This technological readiness creates a historic window to build a system that is decentralised, intelligent, and focused on proactive wellness.

This project's "Made in Australia" guidance directly aligns with the national strategic priority of building sovereign capability in important sectors. The COVID-19 pandemic highlighted the vulnerabilities of global supply chains, particularly for medical devices. 2 By establishing a domestic, end-to-end manufacturing capability for a Class II medical device, this initiative not only enhances health security but also stimulates a high-value, advanced manufacturing sector within Australia. 3 This creates highly skilled jobs, fosters local innovation, and positions Australia as an exporter of next-generation medical technology, rather than an importer. This framing strengthens the case for government support and non-dilutive funding, aligning the project with national programs like the Modern Manufacturing Initiative. 2

## 1.2 The Value Proposition: Democratising Longevity

The core value proposition of the Aura Geode ecosystem is the radical democratisation of access to advanced longevity technology. Hyperbaric Oxygen Therapy is currently an expensive and specialised treatment largely confined to clinical settings, with a typical 120-hour protocol costing upwards of AUD $12,000. This project fundamentally disrupts that model.

## Disrupting the Healthcare Model

Through the group-sharing cooperative structure, the individual cost of completing the 120-hour Aura Genesis Protocol is designed to fall by over 90% at scale, from the AUD $12,000 benchmark to a target of AUD $1,000 per person. This dramatic cost reduction is achieved by transforming a high-cost capital good into a shared community utility, making a powerful preventative health intervention accessible to a vastly larger segment of the Australian population.

## From Patient to Member-Owner

The project fundamentally reframes the user's relationship with healthcare technology. A participant is not a passive "patient" or "customer" but an active "member-owner" of the cooperative that owns the device. This is more than a semantic distinction; it redefines the entire dynamic from a top-down delivery of services to a bottom-up engagement in a shared economic and wellness enterprise. This dual value proposition-offering both tangible health outcomes and a stake in an empowered, sovereign community-is a potent driver for adoption in an era marked by a search for both personal well-being and more equitable economic models.

## Section II: The Aura Geode Ecosystem Architecture

The Aura Geode ecosystem is a deeply integrated platform designed for national scale. Its architecture is built upon four distinct but interdependent pillars that work in concert to create a resilient, self-sustaining, and regenerative system for preventative health.

## 2.1 The Four Pillars of a Regenerative System

A clear understanding of the system's components and their interdependencies is helpful for all stakeholders involved in its deployment.

## Pillar 1: The Aura Geode (Hardware)

The physical nexus of the user experience is the Aura Geode, a medical-grade hyperbaric oxygen therapy (HBOT) device. It will be available in two primary forms: a mass-produced, steel-frame unit engineered for accessibility and affordability, and a luxury "Crystalline Geode" variant designed to build a powerful brand narrative and serve as a unique data-gathering platform through its "Geopoetic Alchemy". The device is the tangible infrastructure that delivers the therapeutic protocols and captures the raw data for the digital twin.

## Pillar 2: The Cooperative Model (Business Structure)

The foundational business and legal structure is the member-owned and controlled Distributing Co-operative, established under Australia's Co-operatives National Law (CNL). This model socialises the significant capital cost of the hardware, transforming an expensive medical device into an affordable, shared community asset. This structure dramatically reduces the financial barrier to entry for individuals and aligns the incentives of all participants towards collective growth and mutual benefit.

## Pillar 3: The Aura of Intelligence (Software)

The intelligence layer is a sophisticated Artificial Intelligence (AI) and digital twin architecture that personalises each user's 120-hour therapeutic journey, the "Aura Genesis Protocol". This system captures invaluable biometric and subjective data through a privacy-preserving, federated learning model. This ensures that while the global AI model learns from the collective data of all users, each individual's raw data remains secure and under their sovereign control, never leaving their local device.

## Pillar 4: The GAJRA Earth DAO (Governance & Finance)

The Global Association for Joyful Responsible Abundance (GAJRA) Earth DAO provides the Web3-native governance and financial layer for the entire ecosystem. It is responsible for managing the $2.5 billion treasury, providing strategic subsidies to fund the ecosystem's growth, and pioneering a novel "Regenerative Asset Class"-the Community-Hour (C-Hour)-designed to value and reward uncompensated community contributions.

## 2.2 System Interdependencies and Flywheel Effect

These four pillars are not siloed; they are designed to interact in a synergistic loop that creates a powerful, self-reinforcing growth mechanism, often referred to as a flywheel effect. This dynamic is central to the project's long-term sustainability and scalability.

The economic flywheel is initiated and sustained by the GAJRA Earth DAO's treasury. A portion of the treasury's assets is actively deployed in established Decentralised Finance (DeFi) protocols to generate a consistent yield. This yield is then programmatically channeled into a subsidy fund. This fund directly lowers the cost of entry for new members by co-funding a portion of the hardware cost or subsidising the interest rates on the micro-loans provided through the Cooperative Model.

This subsidy accelerates adoption, making it easier for new cooperative groups to form and acquire an Aura Geode unit. The resulting increase in deployed units leads to a greater volume and diversity of data being generated during the Aura Genesis Protocols. This richer dataset, processed via the federated learning architecture, significantly enhances the predictive power and personalisation capabilities of the Aura of Intelligence AI.

A more effective and intelligent AI, in turn, increases the value and utility of the entire network, delivering better health outcomes and a more compelling user experience. This enhanced utility drives further demand and increases the intrinsic value of the ecosystem's native governance token, GAJRA. A stronger and more valuable token ultimately strengthens the DAO's treasury, allowing it to generate greater yields and provide larger subsidies, thus completing and accelerating the virtuous cycle. This integrated architecture ensures that financial success is directly coupled with network growth and improved health outcomes for the community.

## Section III: National Manufacturing and Supply Chain Strategy

The successful deployment of 70,000 Aura Geode units requires a sophisticated and resilient national manufacturing strategy. This strategy is architected around a parallel, two-stream framework built on a new philosophy: building sovereign capacity for a specific mission, achieving market saturation, and then repurposing that capacity for the next community-driven innovation. 1

## 3.1 The Parallel Manufacturing Framework: Independent Production Streams

This strategy abandons a traditional, interdependent supply chain in favor of two self-sufficient, parallel production streams designed for speed and resilience.

## Stream 1: Immediate Decentralised Production (Regional Workshops)

The first priority is speed and immediate impact. This will be achieved by establishing a network of small, agile, and easily deployable workshops in regional centres across Australia.

- Role: The express purpose of these workshops is to function as complete, start-to-finish manufacturing pipelines, capable of producing 1 to 10 finished Aura Geode units per day per location. They will serve as hubs for fabrication, assembly, testing, and local distribution, creating short-term contract jobs that require rapid, on-the-job training. The focus is on empowering local communities and kick-starting the national rollout without delay.
- Processes: These workshops will be equipped with the necessary metalworking equipment to build a unit from raw materials to final product. To ensure quality and speed for the important pressure vessel fabrication, they will utilise pre-designed jigs and patterns combined with collaborative robotic (cobot) welding stations. This approach allows a person with a steady hand and minimal training to produce consistent, high-quality welds, overcoming skilled labour bottlenecks. Final assembly of electronics and life support systems, along with pressure testing, will be completed on-site.
- Location Strategy: Workshops will be rapidly established across the country, with a focus on regional areas to stimulate local economies and accelerate the distribution of units to a wide geographic area from the outset.

## Stream 2: Parallel Industrial Scale-Up (Metropolitan Foundries for Cyclical Production)

Running concurrently with, and independent of, the decentralised rollout, the second stream focuses on establishing mission-specific, high-volume manufacturing capacity.

- Role: The goal is to establish a large-scale "Foundry" in or near every major Australian city. These facilities are not designed for permanent, single-product manufacturing. Instead, they are built for a specific mission: to achieve market saturation for the Aura Geode by functioning as a complete, start-to-finish production pipeline capable of producing hundreds of units per day. Once the mission is complete, this industrial capacity will be repurposed for the next community-required system, creating an adaptable sovereign asset rather than a static factory. 1
- Processes: Each Foundry will be a self-sufficient, end-to-end production line. They will leverage full-scale industrial automation, including robotic welding lines and CNC machining centres, to fabricate, assemble, test, and finish complete Aura Geode units at high volume and maximum efficiency. 9
- Location Strategy: Foundries will be established in major metropolitan hubs like Brisbane, Sydney, Melbourne, Perth, and Adelaide, leveraging existing industrial infrastructure, logistics networks, and skilled labour pools. 3

This parallel framework ensures that both regional and metropolitan areas have independent, complete production capabilities, maximising speed and resilience while strategically building an adaptable national manufacturing base for future community-driven innovation.

## 3.2 Design for Manufacturing and Assembly (DFMA): The Important Path to Affordability

The target unit cost of AUD $35,000 is a ceiling, not a floor. The benchmark set by comparable, fully-featured Chinese-made units available for approximately AUD $10,000 demonstrates that extreme cost efficiency is achievable. 1 This price point is impossible without a rigorous and uncompromising focus on Design for Manufacturing and Assembly (DFMA) from the very outset. 15 The design of the mass-produced steel-frame unit could be fundamentally optimised for simplified, rapid production using both basic workshop tools and full-scale automation.

The first major budgetary allocation within the R&D stream will be to commission a dedicated DFMA task force. This team will consist of industrial designers, mechanical and materials engineers, and manufacturing process experts. 20 Their sole guidance will be to value-engineer every component and assembly process of the standard Aura Geode unit to aggressively pursue a target production cost well below the AUD $35,000 ceiling, while maintaining absolute compliance with all safety and regulatory standards, particularly AS 1210. This process will involve minimising part counts, standardising components, designing for robotic welding and simplified manual assembly, and reducing material waste. 15

## 3.3 Supply Chain Architecture

A resilient and cost-effective supply chain is important to the success of the manufacturing operation. The strategy is built on a detailed Bill of Materials (BOM) and a multi-pronged sourcing approach guided by the principle of "make what makes sense here."

- Bill of Materials (BOM): A preliminary, detailed BOM will be developed based on the Aura Geode's technical specifications, providing a granular view of every component required. This BOM will be the foundational tool for the supply chain team.
- Sourcing Strategy: The strategy is to manufacture the heavy, bulky, and difficult-to-ship components-namely the steel pressure vessel-as close to the final point of use as possible within the independent regional and metropolitan hubs. Smaller, standardised, high-volume components such as valves, hoses, electronics, and fasteners will be sourced from the most efficient and cost-effective suppliers globally, including China. A dual-sourcing strategy will be implemented for all important components to mitigate risk.
- Strategic Partnerships: The supply chain team will proactively engage with and establish long-term partnerships with key Australian and international suppliers of specialised, medical-grade components. This includes manufacturers of oil-less air compressors suitable for human occupancy, high-flow oxygen concentrators, and medical-grade electronic components and sensors. 26

## 3.4 Workforce Development: The TAFE Partnership Program

The deployment and long-term maintenance of 70,000 complex medical devices necessitates the creation of a large, skilled national workforce. The parallel manufacturing model requires two distinct but complementary skill sets: a rapidly trained workforce for the regional workshops, and highly specialised technicians for the metropolitan Foundries. Australia's TAFE (Technical and Further Education) system is perfectly positioned to be the primary partner in developing this workforce. 35

The core skills required for both streams align directly with nationally recognised TAFE qualifications. The Certificate III in Engineering - Fabrication Trade (Boilermaking/Welding) provides the useful competencies in metal fabrication, welding, assembly, and interpreting technical drawings required to assemble and service the Aura Geode units. 41 The Certificate IV in Engineering Drafting provides the advanced CAD skills necessary for both the design phase and the operation of CNC equipment in the Foundries. 46

The proposed action plan is to establish the "Aura Geode Apprenticeship Program," a formal partnership co-designed with TAFE institutions across Australia, starting with TAFE Queensland. This program will create a direct training-to-employment pipeline. The regional workshops will serve as ideal environments for rapid, on-the-job training and apprenticeships, providing the crucial hands-on component of their qualification. This symbiotic relationship ensures the project has a sustainable source of skilled labour, while simultaneously creating valuable employment and training opportunities in regional communities across the country.

## Section V: Phased National Rollout and Go-to-Market Plan

The deployment of 70,000 Aura Geode units across Australia will be executed via a pragmatic, multi-year phased rollout. This strategy is designed to de-risk the project by validating the technology, business model, and clinical protocols in a controlled environment before scaling to full national coverage.

## 5.1 5-Year Phased Deployment Schedule

The national rollout is structured as a five-year plan with clear geographical and production milestones.

- Year 1: Queensland Pilot Program & Clinical Validation. The initial phase will be centred in South-East Queensland. The focus will be on establishing the first regional workshop, deploying the first 100-200 units, and executing the important Phase 1 and 2 clinical trials with local partners. This phase is designed to validate the entire ecosystem-the hardware's performance, the software's usability, the cooperative model's appeal, and the clinical protocols' efficacy-in a real-world setting.
- Years 2-3: Eastern Seaboard Expansion. Following a successful pilot, the project will scale rapidly along the eastern seaboard. This phase will see the commissioning of the first metropolitan Foundries and the expansion of the regional workshop network in New South Wales and Victoria. Production will ramp up significantly, targeting the deployment of 5,000 to 10,000 units per year to build market momentum in the most populous states.
- Years 4-5: Full National Coverage. With the manufacturing processes optimised and the go-to-market model proven, the final phase will focus on achieving full national coverage. The network of workshops and Foundries will be expanded to South Australia, Western Australia, and other territories. Production will reach its peak capacity to ensure the 70,000-unit target is met by the end of Year 5.

## 5.2 The Clinical & Regulatory Gateway

Securing regulatory approval is the single most important gateway to market access and long-term viability. The strategy is to proactively and rigorously navigate the Australian regulatory landscape.

- TGA Pathway: The Aura Geode system, comprising both hardware and software (SaMD), will be classified as a Class IIa or potentially Class IIb medical device. The project will follow the TGA's formal conformity assessment process to achieve inclusion on the Australian Register of Therapeutic Goods (ARTG). The budget could account for all associated TGA fees, including application, conformity assessment, and annual listing
- charges. 51 A foundational requirement for this process is the establishment and maintenance of a comprehensive Quality Management System (QMS) compliant with the international standard ISO 13485. 59
- HREC Approval: All clinical research involving human participants could receive prior approval from a registered Human Research Ethics Committee (HREC). The HREC submission for the initial Queensland trial will require meticulous preparation, with a particular focus on addressing the complex ethical considerations of obtaining informed consent from vulnerable populations, such as individuals with dementia.

The initial clinical trial focused on dementia care is not merely a regulatory hurdle; it is the central pillar of the project's entire go-to-market strategy. A successful trial will provide the robust clinical evidence needed for TGA approval. Beyond this, it will generate powerful, positive public relations and establish the system's credibility within the mainstream medical community. This clinical validation will be the key to unlocking the "middle-out" distribution channel by forging partnerships with established wellness centres, medical clinics, and aged care providers who require evidence-based solutions. Therefore, the successful execution of the clinical trial is the most important marketing, business development, and regulatory activity in the project's first year.

## 5.3 Activating the Cooperative Growth Engine

The primary driver of grassroots adoption is the innovative cooperative business model, which is designed to be a self-reinforcing growth engine.

- The Microfinance Model: The high capital cost of the hardware is made accessible through a group-loan model inspired by microfinance principles. Founding cohorts, typically comprising 4 to 7 individuals, will collectively apply for a loan to cover the cost of their HBOT unit, with an initial per-person contribution of around AUD $5,000. This leverages social collateral and shared responsibility to ensure repayment.
- The Patronage Rebate System: This is the core mechanism that transforms members into evangelists. In a distributing co-operative, any financial surplus (profit) is distributed back to members in proportion to their use of the co-operative's services-a concept known as patronage. In this model, the surplus is generated from the operational fees paid by all subsequent members who join the co-op to use the device. As a co-op grows its user base, its surplus increases, resulting in larger patronage rebates for the founding members. These rebates can be directly applied to pay down their initial loan, creating a direct and tangible financial incentive for every member to actively recruit new participants for their local co-op.
- Community Organizers: This bottom-up growth will be facilitated and supported by a network of trained "Community Organizers." These individuals, compensated by the DAO,

will lead an ethical, education-first approach to member recruitment, hosting informational sessions that explain the science of HBOT, the benefits of the protocol, and the principles of cooperative ownership.

## Section VI: Technology, Data, and Research Integration

The deployment of a national network of 70,000 intelligent medical devices creates both a significant technical challenge and an unprecedented research opportunity. The strategy for managing this digital infrastructure is founded on the principles of user sovereignty, data privacy, and collective benefit.

## 6.1 "Aura of Intelligence" at Scale

The successful operation of the network depends on the ability to securely deploy, maintain, and update the AuraOS software across all 70,000 units.

- Software Deployment and Maintenance: A robust, secure pipeline for over-the-air (OTA) software updates will be established. This will allow for the continuous improvement of the AI models, the introduction of new features, and the deployment of security patches across the entire fleet of devices without requiring physical intervention.
- Data Sovereignty Architecture: The system's architecture is built upon a non-negotiable commitment to user privacy and data sovereignty. This is achieved through a federated learning model combined with Personal Data Vaults. The bulk of a user's sensitive biometric and personal data resides on their local device or in a secure, user-controlled cloud vault. To enable collective intelligence without centralising raw data, the central AI sends generic analysis models to the edge (the user's device) for training. Only the anonymous, aggregated model updates-not the raw data itself-are sent back to improve the global model. This allows the entire network to learn and improve collectively while ensuring each user's personal data remains secure and under their absolute control.

## 6.2 The "Project Aether" National Research Initiative

The network of 70,000 deployed units represents more than just a service delivery platform; it is the world's largest distributed research platform for preventative health and longevity science.

- Vision: Project Aether is the framework for leveraging this platform as a national research asset. Its vision is to create the world's largest-ever dataset on the intersection of hyperbaric conditions, material science, sensory inputs, and human physiological and psychological responses.
- Systematic Data Collection: The system is designed as a scientific instrument from day one. Every variable in the user experience is logged and tagged. This includes the unique material composition of each capsule's shell (via its immutable Material Composition ID or "crystal-print"), the precise atmospheric composition delivered during each session, and the specific sensory inputs (light, sound, scent) used. This environmental data is then correlated with the vast streams of anonymised biometric data (EEG, HRV, etc.) and subjective user-reported outcomes captured during the sessions.
- A National Asset: This vast, longitudinal, and anonymised dataset will be positioned as a national treasure. It offers an unprecedented opportunity to conduct population-scale research into the environmental, lifestyle, and therapeutic factors that influence the health and well-being of the Australian population. This research can provide profound insights for public health policy, preventative medicine, and future medical innovation. The project will seek to partner with national research bodies and pursue non-dilutive funding from government programs, such as the Medical Research Future Fund (MRFF), to support the analysis and stewardship of this invaluable national data resource.

## Section VIII: Strategic Recommendations and Important Path

The successful execution of this ambitious national initiative depends on a disciplined focus on a series of important, interdependent activities. This final section summarises the project's important path and provides a clear, prioritised set of actionable recommendations for the project's leadership over the initial 18-24 month period.

## 8.1 Summary of Important Path Dependencies

The analysis of the integrated project plan reveals a clear important path. All subsequent large-scale activities-including the commissioning of metropolitan manufacturing facilities, the national rollout of regional workshops, and the activation of the cooperative growth model-are fundamentally dependent on the successful completion of two foundational pillars:

1. Design for Manufacturing and Assembly (DFMA) and Prototyping: The financial viability of the entire ecosystem hinges on achieving a highly competitive unit cost. This cannot be an assumption; it could be a validated outcome of a rigorous engineering process. The DFMA phase, culminating in a manufacturing-ready prototype that meets this aggressive cost target, is the useful prerequisite for all manufacturing and supply chain investments.
2. The Queensland Clinical Trial and Regulatory Submission: Market access for the Aura Geode is legally impossible without TGA approval. TGA approval is impossible without robust clinical evidence. Therefore, the successful execution of the phased clinical trial, beginning in Queensland, and the subsequent preparation of the TGA submission package represent the single most important gateway to commercialisation.

These two streams could be pursued in parallel and with the highest priority, as they gate the deployment of the vast majority of the project's capital and the initiation of its revenue-generating activities.

## 8.2 Prioritised Recommendations for the Next 18 Months

To ensure the project is established on a solid foundation and maintains momentum, the following five strategic actions should be executed with immediate effect:

1. Finalise Corporate & Legal Structure: Immediately establish the primary Australian legal entity that will oversee the project. Concurrently, finalise the standardised Model Rules and Disclosure Statement for the Distributing Co-operatives in accordance with the Co-operatives National Law (CNL). This legal scaffolding is helpful for all subsequent commercial and regulatory activities.
2. Commission the DFMA & Prototyping Team: Allocate the first significant tranche of the R&D budget to a dedicated, multi-disciplinary team of engineers, industrial designers, and manufacturing partners. Their primary objective is to deliver a fully costed, manufacturing-ready prototype of the steel-frame Aura Geode unit that is validated to meet the aggressive price point and all AS 1210 safety requirements.
3. Assemble the Queensland Clinical Trial Team: Actively recruit and appoint the key personnel for the initial clinical trial, including a Queensland-based Principal Investigator with expertise in dementia research and an experienced Clinical Trial Coordinator. This team's first task will be to develop the detailed research protocol and begin preparing the HREC submission, while simultaneously forging the necessary partnerships with local dementia care organisations for participant recruitment.
4. Initiate TGA Engagement: Formally appoint the Australian Sponsor required for ARTG inclusion. The sponsor's first action will be to request a pre-submission meeting with the TGA. The goal of this meeting is to present the Aura Geode system, discuss its intended use and features, and gain clarity on its likely risk classification (Class IIa/IIb) and the specific clinical evidence that will be required for a successful conformity assessment.
5. Develop DAO Treasury Management Protocols: Formalise, audit, and implement the operational security protocols for managing the $2.5 billion GAJRA Earth DAO treasury. This includes establishing institutional-grade, multi-signature wallet procedures, finalising the asset allocation strategy, and conducting rigorous security audits on any smart contracts that will govern the initial yield-farming and subsidy-distribution mechanisms. Capital preservation and operational security could be the paramount principles.

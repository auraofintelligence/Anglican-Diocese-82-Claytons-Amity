## Alpha Infinity Foundation:

Owns all foundational IP; licenses to Aura, GAJRA, QI. Cannot be owned by SSWF.

## Aura of Intelligence:

Builds clinics and services. May lease space or license content to Straddie via SSWF, but remains a globalpurpose entity.

## GAJRA Earth:

Runs global DAO, Earth diplomacy and tourism. May sponsor local festivals or ceremonies but retains global IP and tokens.

## 500 Queens Venture Capital (500QVC):

Funds planetary innovations. May invest in Straddie projects, but retains its own governance, shareholders and global reach.

## Straddie Sovereign Wealth Fund (SSWF):

Holds only Straddie land, energy infrastructure, tunnels, sports facilities, and clean energy assets. Firewall prevents ownership/control over Aura, Alpha, GAJRA, or 500QVC.

## Straddie Employment and Training Company (SETCo):

Deploys locals across all companies needing staff or training. Operates as Straddie's HR + Talent Onboarding DAO.

## Quandamooka Intelligence:

Delivers software and AI for Aura, Clean Energy, Film Festival etc. Co-op keeps its own governance, but interoperates with grants, tools, and events.

## Sandworm Subterranean Systems:

Tunnels the island to deliver trade routes, shelter, sand assets, and underground zones. Co-develops with Clean Energy, Fungineer, Deep Greenhouses, and SSWF.

## Straddie Clean Energy Co:

Provides electricity and sand batteries. Island systems owned by SSWF; export energy/IP owned by Clean Energy Co.

## Sandy Sports Club:

Fully owned/managed within SSWF. Youth programs, sports tourism, wellness integration.

- Vision : Enhance the quality of life, autonomy, and dignity of individuals with dementia through advanced AI and smart device integration.
- Mission : Create an adaptable AI solution that aligns with the evolving needs of its users and integrates emerging technological advancements.

## 4. Market Analysis

- Target Market : Early onset dementia patients (plus their families, caregivers)
- Market Size :
- o Australian Early Onset Dementia population is estimated to be 28,650 people
- o Australian total population of Dementia cases is estimated at over 400,000
- o USA Early Onset Dementia population is estimated to be 220,000 to 640,000
- o UK Early Onset Dementia population is estimated to be 70,800 people
- o In 2019, Dementia cost the global economy approximately 1.3 trillion USD
- o The total global estimate for Dementia is 55.2 million people x $23,796 USD
- o Annually there are over 10 million new diagnosed cases of Dementia globally
- Competitive Analysis : Overview of existing solutions and their gaps.
- o Companies (Pharmaceuticals, Biotech, and Technology):

- Roche/Genentech : Working on dementia and Alzheimer's treatments.
- Merck & Co. : Conducts research on Alzheimer's drugs.

## o Non-Profit Organisations and Advocacy Groups:

- Alzheimer's Association : A leading voluntary health organisation in Alzheimer's care, support, and research.
- Alzheimer's Research UK : The UK's leading dementia research charity.
- Alzheimer's Society (UK) : Provides care, funds research, and advocates for policy on dementia.
- Alzheimer's Disease International (ADI) : An international federation of Alzheimer's organisations.
- Lewy Body Dementia Association : Focuses on Lewy body dementias.
- UsAgainstAlzheimer's : An advocacy organisation and network pushing for a cure.
- Dementia Australia : Represents people affected by dementia in Australia.
- Cure Alzheimer's Fund : Funds research for Alzheimer's disease.
- BrightFocus Foundation : Supports research on Alzheimer's, glaucoma, and macular degeneration.
- ACT on Alzheimer's : A collaboration of organisations and individuals working to prepare Minnesota for the impacts of Alzheimer's disease.

## o Research and Academic Institutions:

- The Alzheimer's Disease Neuroimaging Initiative (ADNI) : A large research initiative helping to define the progression of Alzheimer's disease.
- Banner Alzheimer's Institute : Engaged in research and offers patient care.
- The Knight Alzheimer's Disease Research Centre at Washington University : Conducts a range of Alzheimer's-related research.

This list is by no means exhaustive, but it gives an overview of the significant players in the field of dementia. Each country often has its national dementia and Alzheimer's association, and there are countless smaller organisations, research institutes, and companies worldwide working on the problem from various angles.
